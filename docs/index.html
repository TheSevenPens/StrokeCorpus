What the quality tags mean

Every take's contact data is sound. What the tags are about is the rest: the channels a take's format carried, and whether its record of the pen in the air can be trusted. The pen's own timestamp is a packet counter that advances a flat 4.166 ms per delivered packet and runs at 0.673 of real time, so a take with no host clock cannot date a reading in real time — and a take whose approach was aged on that counter has an incomplete record of the approach.

One hand, one Wacom Cintiq 24, one backend so far. The repository holds the raw JSON, the format, and how to contribute a recording of your own.
